# ByNext

> ByNext is a US-based on-demand laundry, dry cleaning, and home cleaning pickup & delivery service. Available 7 days a week (8am–8pm), with 24–48 hour turnaround. App-based booking on iOS and Android. Average order cost: $20–$50. Pricing models: pay-per-pound or all-inclusive subscription per bag. No hidden fees. Free pickup and delivery included. 46,000+ customer reviews. 25+ store locations across the US.
